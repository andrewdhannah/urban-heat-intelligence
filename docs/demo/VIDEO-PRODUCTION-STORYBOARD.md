## Production Philosophy

> **AI footage illustrates. Real evidence proves.**

The strongest thing about UHI is that the data, application, evidence, maps, and provenance are real. The video must visually reinforce that — not make judges wonder what was generated.

**Visual ratio:**
- 65–70% actual UHI/product footage
- 15–20% real/stock human-interest footage
- 5–10% diagrams/source material
- 5–10% Seedance-generated connective imagery

**Visual vocabulary:** Dark graphite/navy background, warm thermal orange/red highlights, white/off-white typography, restrained data labels, subtle grid/geospatial lines. Closer to Bloomberg / modern weather intelligence than AI startup promo.

## Stock Footage Shopping List

### Look For

- Phoenix skyline summer
- Arizona urban aerial
- Heat shimmer street
- Pedestrians in heat
- Bus stop / public transit heat
- Construction worker or outdoor worker
- Sparse urban shade
- Asphalt parking lot
- Tree canopy vs non-canopy contrast
- Urban park
- Sun over city skyline

### Avoid

- Obviously non-Phoenix downtown footage
- Generic desert dunes
- Dramatic wildfire imagery
- Staged people collapsing from heat
- Footage implying actual emergencies that you're not documenting

## Audio

### Music

- Restrained documentary/news music bed
- Subtle pulse, low percussion, atmospheric texture
- No inspirational corporate ukulele
- No huge cinematic trailer drums
- Reduce music considerably during evidence reveal — the voice is the story

### Voiceover

- **Preferred:** Owner's voice — makes submission feel authored rather than manufactured
- **Acceptable:** High-quality AI voice — conversational and documentary-like, NOT hyper-polished commercial announcer
